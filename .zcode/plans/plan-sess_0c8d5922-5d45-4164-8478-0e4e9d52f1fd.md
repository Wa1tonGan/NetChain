## Design answer (your Q2 sub-question)
Yes — **the platform wallet (`0xabc67fa3…`) owns the shared escrow pool + AuthorityCap**. That does NOT mean the platform pays: via the zkLogin path (`commit_as_buyer`), each user's USDC is deducted **from their own logged-in zk wallet** at commit, locked into the platform-owned escrow, then released at settle to the Gonka-chosen provider + platform fee.
